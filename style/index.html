a little about lila

For most of her career, Lila has helped women fall back in love with their closets and their style.

She spent years running her own travel agency, a decade as a magazine editor, and sixteen years as a buyer and stylist at Vesta's in Little Rock.

Edited by Lila is her next chapter — personal styling, closet and travel edits, and curated experiences, built around the way you actually live.
